HOW TO PLAY
PVP
Last ship alive wins the round; first to your chosen ROUND WINS takes the match. Bullets cost 1 HP and knock you back; ammo is limited. Everyone is frozen during the 3-2-1.
CO-OP
Team survival against endless waves. Enemies spawn in gradually and try to encircle you. A boss comes every 5 waves, and each boss unlocks a new enemy type. Every wave grants +50% ammo. Only bosses give health (+2 HP, +1 life).
SOLO
Co-op for one — same waves and bosses, with the lives you set in Health.
VS AI
A 1v1 duel vs the computer. On the Difficulty page set its skill (Easy → Impossible) and its HP. Same rules as PvP.
SHOOTING RANGE
Practice with infinite ammo and a bullseye that respawns on each hit. The HUD tracks fire rate, hits/sec, and accuracy; RESET clears the stats. TARGET toggles random vs fixed spawn.
CONTROLS
Pick your input on the Players and Controls page: Keyboard (WASD / IJKL / Arrows + a fire key), Mouse (move with keys, aim + click to fire), or Touch (on-screen joystick + fire). Mouse runs aren't ranked.
HEALTH, LIVES & PICKUPS
HP is a hit count (1–9); every hit knocks you back. Amber crates refill ~half a magazine; heart crates (max 2 per wave) give +1 HP when you're hurt.
MAPS
PvP and Shooting Range only: Black Space (blank), Navigation (a turret targets you), Rainbow (edges wrap around), Portal (step-through portals), or Custom — build walls, turrets, and portals and save them.
SETTINGS
Open the gear (top-right) or press ESC anytime to adjust sound and music. Mid-game it pauses with Resume / Exit to main menu.
